About this catalog

Inzegane Platform is a research catalog for discovering electronics products and comparing recorded wholesale price information from suppliers in Inezgane and the surrounding Souss-Massa area.

What the current dataset contains

What the catalog provides

The catalog brings together product records, supplier records, and supplier offers that were documented in a research snapshot. Each offer records a price, its VAT basis, quantity conditions where known, and the date the information was observed and last verified.

  • Products describe the item itself: brand, model, category, specifications, and packaging.
  • Suppliers describe the businesses that were documented, including the contact details recorded at the time.
  • Offers link a product to a supplier with a recorded price and conditions. Prices belong to offers, never to products.
  • Price history and availability checks are dated observations kept for context.

How to use it

  1. Find a product with the product search or by browsing categories.
  2. Open the product to compare the recorded offers from different suppliers side by side, checking the VAT basis (HT or TTC) and the minimum order quantity.
  3. Check each offer's verification date and status. Offers marked “Historical” are older than the freshness window for their category.
  4. Contact the supplier to confirm the current price and stock before buying.

Catalog information is not a live market price

The records were transcribed from a research document and have not been independently verified by this site. A price that was verified on a past date is a recorded price; it does not mean the price is still valid today. The price verification methodology explains how dates and statuses are interpreted.

The catalog covers only the suppliers and products documented in the snapshot. It does not represent all local suppliers or the entire Inezgane market, and it does not imply any partnership with, or endorsement by, the businesses listed.
