About the current dataset

Recorded fields and what they mean

Recorded source

Every offer, price observation, and availability check references at least one source record: a field observation, B2B price sheet, official portal, phone inquiry, public catalog, or online storefront. Source records include a title, publisher, access date, optional URL, and a reliability rating stated in the research document. The site shows these as recorded; it has not checked the sources itself.

Date the information was collected

An offer's observed date (observed_at) is when the price was first documented. A source's accessed date is when it was consulted. When a date is missing, the site shows “Unknown” rather than estimating it.

Date of the latest verification

An offer's last verified date (last_verified_at) is the most recent date the source reports re-checking the price. If it is missing, the observed date is used. A collection or verification date in the past never means that the price is still current.

Availability checks

Availability checks are separate, dated stock observations (checked_at) with a method such as shelf inspection or inventory count, and sometimes a counted quantity. They describe stock on that date only.

Historical observations versus current offers

Price history entries are past observations kept for context and trend reading. The offer record holds the latest recorded terms. Neither is a live quote.

How the site labels prices

For each offer, the site calculates the number of days since the last verification date and compares it with a freshness window that depends on the product category:

  • Recently verified: verified within the last 7 days.
  • Needs re-check: older than 7 days but within the category window: 14 days for mobile phones and accessories, 21 days for refurbished laptops, and 30 days for other categories.
  • Historical: older than the category window. The price is shown as a “recorded price” and stock claims are treated as unknown.
  • Verification date unknown: no usable date, or a date in the future. Price and stock are unconfirmed.

A price type such as “Wholesale price (verified at source)” describes how the source obtained the price. It does not override the date-based status above.

Missing, stale, or unverified information

  • Missing values are shown as “Not recorded” or omitted. They are never filled in or estimated.
  • Amounts are not normalized across VAT bases (HT, TTC, exempt), price bases, or quantity tiers, so the site does not rank offers by price.
  • Supplier contact details are reproduced as recorded and may be outdated.
  • The site is static: it does not receive live updates, and changes made in the administration preview are not shown on public pages.
