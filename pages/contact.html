Contact and corrections

Help keep the catalog accurate by reporting incorrect product information, outdated prices, or wrong supplier details.

This site has no contact form, email address, or phone line. It is a static website with no message-delivery backend, so it cannot receive messages directly.

Reports are handled through the project's public issue tracker on GitHub.

Open a report on GitHub View existing reports

A free GitHub account is required to open an issue. Issues are public: do not include personal data, phone numbers, or private business information.

What to include in a report

  • The page address (URL) of the product, supplier, or offer, which includes its identifier, for example ?id=off_inz_x14_c_01.
  • What is wrong: the field (price, VAT basis, stock, address, phone, specification…) and the value you believe is correct.
  • When and how you observed the correct information (date, in-store visit, published price list, supplier website).
  • A public source link, if one exists.

How reports are used

Reports are reviewed before any change. Corrections are applied to the source dataset and published with the next catalog update; nothing changes on the site immediately. A report is not treated as verification on its own.

Contacting suppliers

To ask about a current price or stock, contact the supplier directly. Supplier detail and offer pages show phone, WhatsApp, or website links only where they were recorded in the dataset. These details may be outdated and are not managed by this site.
